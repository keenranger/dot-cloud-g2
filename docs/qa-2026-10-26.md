One initialization of the actual packaged WASM/KWS/VAD processed 16 kHz mono PCM16 in 640-byte chunks, with unchanged assets and thresholds. Installed offline Samantha English TTS generated six nonsensitive fixtures without playback/provider calls.

| Phrase | Observed wake/capture |
| --- | --- |
| Hey dots. What time is it? (175 WPM) | 1 wake, 1 capture, 2.12 s |
| Hey dots. Tell me the time. (140 WPM) | 1 wake, 1 capture, 2.44 s |
| Hey dots. | 1 wake, no streamed audio/capture within fixture window |
| Hello there. What time is it? | No wake |
| Hey dog. What time is it? | No wake |
| Eight seconds silence | No wake |

All six cases had zero engine errors. Cue-only had four trailing seconds, shorter than the six-second no-speech cap; it is not timeout proof. This single synthetic voice corpus does not establish population accuracy, Korean pronunciation, noise robustness, microphone performance or realtime latency.
